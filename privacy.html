Privacy Policy

Parz Das Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner to post the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok account.

Data accessed: the TikTok account's basic profile (open ID, username) and an access token, used solely to upload and publish videos the owner selects.

Storage: tokens and video files are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Revoking access: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
